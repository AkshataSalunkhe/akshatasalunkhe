About Me

I am a data professional with experience across machine learning, analytics engineering, data quality, cloud data platforms, and business intelligence.

Currently, I work at NextEra Energy, where I apply data science, analytics, and data governance practices to help build reliable, scalable, and trusted data solutions in the energy domain.

Before that, I worked with DHL Express, where I built operational analytics solutions, automated reporting workflows, and created business intelligence dashboards that supported faster and more informed decision-making across logistics operations.

I enjoy working on problems where data, technology, and business strategy meet — from preparing clean and reliable datasets to building models, dashboards, and decision-support solutions that create measurable impact.
